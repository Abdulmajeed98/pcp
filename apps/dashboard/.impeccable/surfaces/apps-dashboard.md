# The dashboard

**Scope and mode.** Every screen of the Admin console (Vendors, Catalog, Missing specifications, Part Requests, Platform Rate, Expiring Offers, Referrals) and of the Vendor portal (Offers, Part Requests, the sheet upload and its report, Referrals, contact details). Mode: Operate.

**Audience and job.** The Admin, the platform's owner, on a phone in a Vendor's shop and at a desk, in English. A Vendor's staff, on a phone behind the counter between customers, in Kurdish, Arabic or English, keeping Offers current and confirmed. Both come back often and want the work done in as few taps as possible.

**Proof.** Real figures only: the Platform Rate as the Admin set it, each Offer's price and the day it was confirmed, counts that come from the data.

**Constraints.** PRODUCT.md, the root DESIGN.md and this app's DESIGN.md. WCAG 2.2 AA in both schemes, 360 pixels wide first, controls 44 pixels on a touchscreen. The Vendor portal right to left in Kurdish and Arabic.

**Chosen direction.** Rate Board, picked by the owner on 15 October 2026 in the second hand, after re-rolling the first. The memorable moment: the numbers people keep set on dark plates in amber figures, each with its date.

**Unresolved.** Which counts on each Admin screen earn a plate; how a Vendor's sheet upload report shows its changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The numbers a Vendor and the Admin keep are set like a money changer's board, plain figures on dark plates, refreshed by hand and dated, on a quiet working screen in the manner of Linear. It refuses the grey, cold admin console and the dashboard of decorative charts.

OWN-WORLD: A warm page and ink, Plate Graphite with Plate Amber figures in Geist Mono, Action Red only where something needs doing, Geist and Noto Sans Arabic, 6-pixel corners, dense rows, borders not shadows.

STORY: The Admin sees what needs action first, catalog gaps, expiring Offers and the rate's age, and acts in a tap; a Vendor sees each Offer's price and how fresh it is, confirms them all at once, and trusts the dates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The Admin console's Catalog at 360 pixels: a quiet ink top bar with the sections behind a menu button; the search field and the Category filter, 44 pixels high; a plate with how many Parts match; then dense rows of Category icon, Part name and manufacturer, each Part missing a needed specification ending in a small amber plate with how many.

FORM: Rate Board, first of seven grounded candidates in the second hand, ordered by resonance, picked by the owner; seed 9feb5d5d, re-roll 1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

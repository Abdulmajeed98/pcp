# 04: Keep or drop the Rate Board

**Spec:** [the redesign spec](../spec.md)

**What to build:** The owner decides whether the dashboard's idea, the Rate Board, stays: figures on graphite plates, taken from the boards of Erbil's money changers. It is the kind of concept born of its subject that Taste asks for, so the question is whether the idea is at fault or only how it was carried out. The owner reads ticket 02's findings on it and decides, and ticket 05 deals its directions from that decision.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** resolved

- [x] The findings of ticket 02 marked as against the idea and as against its execution are shown to the owner side by side, in the built-in browser, with the screens they are about.
- [x] The owner's decision is one of: kept as it is, kept with its execution changed (and what must change), or dropped. It is recorded under Comments with the owner's word.
- [x] What ticket 05 must hold to is written as one line for its brief: the idea to keep, or that the draw is free of it.

## Comments

### 21 October 2026: the owner's decision

Ticket 02's findings on the Rate Board were set side by side, against the idea in one column and against its execution in the other, beside the captures they are about (the portal's Offers, the Catalog, a Part, the Vendors and the Offer form, in the dark and the light scheme), at `.taste-skill/redesign-04/index.html` in the owner's checkout, opened in the built-in browser before the owner was asked. No finding is against the idea outright: Taste supports it, Impeccable and Taste limit its reach to prices and the rate, and Taste names the Offer form and the Platform Rate screen as where it would earn its place. Nine findings are against its execution.

Asked whether the board stays, the owner answered **"Keep, change execution"**. Asked what must change, the owner chose all four:

- **"Prices only, one lead".** Plates only on prices and the rate, never on counts (the Catalog's, a Part's, the Vendors'); fewer plates per Offer; and one larger leading plate per screen where a figure leads.
- **"Quiet units, real comma".** "$" and "IQD" as a smaller muted suffix, not bold amber; the figure set so that the comma takes no whole cell.
- **"Dated, stock-aware plate".** The plate carries its date beside it, and an out-of-stock Offer's plate is muted.
- **"Holds in both schemes".** The plate reads as a plate on the night page, not a grey chip, and is not the darkest mass on the light page; the warm page is cooled to a low-chroma stone.

**The line for ticket 05's brief:** keep the Rate Board, figures on plates from Erbil's money changers' boards, but only on prices and the rate, one leading plate per screen, units a quiet suffix with a proportional comma, each plate dated and muted when out of stock, and a plate that holds in both schemes on a low-chroma stone page.

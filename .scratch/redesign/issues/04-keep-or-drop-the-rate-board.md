# 04: Keep or drop the Rate Board

**Spec:** [the redesign spec](../spec.md)

**What to build:** The owner decides whether the dashboard's idea, the Rate Board, stays: figures on graphite plates, taken from the boards of Erbil's money changers. It is the kind of concept born of its subject that Taste asks for, so the question is whether the idea is at fault or only how it was carried out. The owner reads ticket 02's findings on it and decides, and ticket 05 deals its directions from that decision.

**Blocked by:** 02

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] The findings of ticket 02 marked as against the idea and as against its execution are shown to the owner side by side, in the built-in browser, with the screens they are about.
- [ ] The owner's decision is one of: kept as it is, kept with its execution changed (and what must change), or dropped. It is recorded under Comments with the owner's word.
- [ ] What ticket 05 must hold to is written as one line for its brief: the idea to keep, or that the draw is free of it.

The development database waited for the owner's yes, and was brought to the reading on 8 November 2026, below.
   - **The count, again.** Read from the development database (read only) and from the import's in-memory run of the pinned commit (below), which agree: 723 of the 3,691 imported motherboards hold an empty list, 36 of them of a socket of 2017 or later (AM4 17, LGA 1200 11, AM5 1, LGA 1700 2, LGA 1851 2, LGA 2066 1, TR4 2, among them the ASRock X570 Taichi and the two ASRock B860 Challenger WiFi boards) and 687 of older sockets, 9 of these with a CPU built in, as ticket 04 measured. **The Standards' findings.**

1. **ADR-0005 is out of date.** Fixed in `08bcecc`: an amendment of 8 November 2026, after the decision and before the options, as ADR-0008 carries its own. 2. **A word to avoid.** Fixed in `b0802fc`: rule 4's test now says the known kit "is of the motherboard's type". The diff's other uses of "fit" are of a Part's size against a case's limit or mounting, as the glossary's own Compatibility Rule and Compatibility Problem use the word (rules 7, 9, 17 and 19, rule 17's test, and the tickets' records of them), or of a value against its specification (the import's "a value that does not fit its specification"), and none says a Part fits a Build, so they stay.
3. **Duplicated drive counting.** Fixed in `416d1ca`: `drivesWhose` in `drives.ts` counts the drives whose value at a path is of a kind, and `driveWhose` one drive, nesting for a kind two values tell. `sataDrives`, rule 14's `m2Drives` and `m2DrivesNotSata` and rule 15's drives of a size are all written with them.
4. **Rule 14's slots.** Fixed in `416d1ca`, through a variant, since it is simpler: `rowsOf`, in the new `reading.ts`, reads a list's rows at a field and names each as the API does, and both `someRow` and its amount beside it, `totalOfRows`, read rows through it, so rule 14's `slotsKeyed` is one call.
5. **`PartOf`.** Fixed in `cc2beeb`: `SpecificationsOf` moved from the statement to `build.ts`, beside the Build, and `PartOf` is a mapped type of `Part<SpecificationsOf<…>>`, still indexed by the Category so that the tests' table of several Categories types its rows.

7. **`Involves`.** Fixed in `cc2beeb`: `PathsRead`, the paths a rule reads by Category, which is true of both `involves` and `readsWhenHeld`.

**The choices made.**

- **Days left (finding 1).** A Vendor's list answers `daysLeft` on each Offer, beside `daysSinceConfirmation`: the whole days of 24 hours from now until the Offer expires, rounded down, `wholeDaysLeft(confirmedAt, now)` in `domain/offer/expiry.ts` from `DAYS_AN_OFFER_STAYS_CURRENT` and `A_DAY`, through `Offer.getDaysLeft(now)`. The boundaries, written first and run red: 14 at the Confirmation, 13 a day later, **3 at exactly 11 days** (not yet about to expire), **2 one millisecond later** (about to expire), 1 at 13 days, 0 from one millisecond after 13 days to the 14-day boundary itself, and 0 for an Expired Offer, which is never below 0. The owner decided that the API answers the days left. The review's findings sketched "days left 3 just after the 11-day mark" as a boundary to pick; rounded down, an Offer about to expire has at most 2 days 23 hours left, so it reads "expires in 2 days", and "expires in 3 days" is never shown. Rounding down is what makes less than one whole day, the last 24 hours, read "expires today", which was the finding, so the boundaries above follow the rule rather than the sketch. An Expired Offer answers 0 rather than null, so the field is a plain number; the portal reads `expired` first. The dashboard's `confirmationOf` reads `daysLeft` and its copy of the 14 days is gone.
- **The catalog search's key (finding 2).** Both keys, as the owner chose. TanStack Query asks a query again by the start of its key, so one key cannot sit under two roots; the search is keyed `['parts', 'offered-by', vendorId, …]`, under the Parts' key, and every change to a Vendor's Offers now goes through one function, `askAgainAfterAChangeToTheOffers(queryClient, vendorId, options)` in the `offer` entity, which asks again the Vendor's Offers, its catalog search, and the Admin's two lists. The four places that changed Offers (adding, saving, removing, confirming all) call it; saving and removing still ask the Vendor's own Offers with `refetchType: 'all'`, and removing still leaves the removed Offer's own query out. So that the `offer` entity can name keys under the Parts' and the Vendors' roots without importing those entities, which the layer rules forbid, `partsKey` and `vendorsKey` move to `shared/api/query-keys.ts`; the `part` and `vendor` entities export them as before.
- **The Admin's lists (finding 11).** Expiring Offers stays under the Vendors' key, so a change to a Vendor still asks it again, and the function above asks it again after any Offer change. The same was true of Missing specifications, which lists Parts that have an Offer, with how many, under the Parts' key, and which no Offer change asked again; it is asked again too. Neither line has a test of its own: TanStack Query's `staleTime` is 0 here, so a screen mounted again asks again anyway, and what changed is which answer shows during that request, which a test could only catch by racing it.
- **The limits (finding 3).** The text stops naming the number, the cheaper honest choice: the number from the API would have needed a new field in every `NOT_VALID` detail, the core's error answer, the description and the client, and would still leave the warranty's hint, shown before any refusal, without its number. The refusals say "The price is over the limit." and "The warranty is over the limit.", the warranty's hint "In whole months.", and Expiring Offers' empty message "No active Vendor has an Offer expired or about to expire." `features/offer-form/model/limits.ts` is gone. The Kurdish and Arabic are drafts under LANG-7. Their words for "the limit", سنووری ڕێگەپێدراو in Kurdish and الحد المسموح in Arabic, are new to the files and not in `words.md`, so they want the owner's reading with the rest of the drafts.
- **The page in the address (finding 4).** `shared/lib/page-in-the-address.ts` gains `pageIn(params)` and `writePageInto(params, page)`, which `usePageInTheAddress` is built on. The Offers screen and the catalog search use them, and so does the Admin console's Catalog (`pages/catalog/model/search.ts`), the third copy of the same parsing, which the finding did not name.
- **The paged lists (finding 5).** `shared/ui/paged-list.tsx` holds `PagedList` (the count on its plate, `aria-busy`, the rows, `PastTheLastPage`, `ListPages`), `SketchedRows` (the sketch of the first answer, each screen giving its row's shape) and `NothingToDo` (the empty state with a circled tick). Each screen keeps its words, its row and its refusal panel; the panel stays on the page because it needs `WhyNotAnswered` from `shared/api`, and it differs only in its title.
- **The digits (finding 6).** One table, `westernDigitsIn` in `shared/lib/western-digits.ts`: Arabic-Indic and Eastern Arabic-Indic digits and the Arabic decimal separator. The Offer form then drops its thousands separators, and the map location reads the Arabic comma as a comma, each in its own file.
- **The WhatsApp link (finding 7).** `whatsAppChatWith(internationalNumber, message?)` in `shared/lib/whatsapp.ts`, used by the hand-over with its message and by Expiring Offers without one. `callTo` stays on the Expiring Offers page, its only user.
- **The expiry's SQL (finding 8).** `expiredAt(now)` and `aboutToExpireAt(now)` at the Offer Store's module level, from `expiry.ts`'s moments; a Vendor's counts and the Vendors with expiring Offers both use them.
- **The visible-Offer rule (finding 9).** `domain/offer/visibility.ts` now states both conditions, `visibleOfferRuleAt(now)` answering `{ vendorActive: true, confirmedFrom }`, and the Store's one `visibleOffer(now)` reads both from it, so the rule is still written once, in the domain, with its query in the Store. Ticket 06's Comments name `visibleIfConfirmedFrom(now)`, which this replaces.
- **The shadowing (finding 10).** The local in `listByVendor` is `theVendors`.

### 18 November 2026: ADR-0008, amended

[ADR-0008](../../../docs/adr/0008-language-in-the-address-and-two-message-formats.md) gains an amendment of 18 November 2026, "a Shared Build's link keeps the rule", after the one of 7 November, and its consequence that left the matter to `sharing` now says that a Shared Build's link keeps the rule: it opens in its sender's language, and opening it is remembered as any Shopper address is (the spec's decision of 17 November 2026, ticket 04's build of it).

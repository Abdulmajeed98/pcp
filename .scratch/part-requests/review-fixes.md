# The code review's fixes: the `part-requests` module

The code review of `part-requests/integration` since `0aa85cc` ran on 18 November 2026, on `0b7a0d3`, along two axes, Standards and Spec. It found four things against the Spec, two partly built and two that looked wrong, and twelve against the Standards, three of them hard (documented standards) and nine judgement calls; the run added three follow-ups for it. The orchestrator's brief, in the owner's checkout at `.taste-skill/part-requests-briefs/review-fixes.md`, said what to do with each, with both reports beside it, and took the decisions that waited on the owner under the owner's standing instruction. This file records how each was dealt with, on the branch `part-requests/review-fixes`, one line or a short paragraph to a finding. It ticks no ticket and leaves every Status line alone; ticket 05 writes the MVP spec's **The part-requests module's conventions**.

**The API, an addition to the spec's table** (Spec finding 1):

| Endpoint | Who may call it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `GET v1/part-requests/{partRequestId}/fulfilment?partId=…` | Admin | What fulfilling the open request with the Part would do, without doing it: `offer`, `created`, `replaced` or `kept`, by the rule in `offer-fulfilment.ts`, and `offerWouldBeExpired`, true for an Offer made or replaced once the request was sent more than 14 days before, never for a kept one. It refuses as a fulfilment does, in its order: 404 `PART_REQUEST_NOT_FOUND`, 400 `PART_REQUEST_RESOLVED`, 404 `PART_NOT_FOUND`; a missing `partId` is 400 `NOT_VALID`, a Vendor's token 403 `ACCESS_DENIED`, no token 401. The client's function is `readPartRequestFulfilment`. |

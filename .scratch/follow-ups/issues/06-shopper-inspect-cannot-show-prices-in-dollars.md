# 06: `pnpm shopper:inspect` cannot show prices in dollars

**Category:** enhancement

**Status:** resolved

**From:** browse's follow-ups list, swept on 7 November 2026: browse ticket 06.

**What happens.** The command takes `--language` and `--scheme` but no currency, so every page it inspects shows prices in dinars. The dollar view, with its own prices and no "≈", never goes before Taste's inspector. The currency is the cookie the frame's switch sets.

**Checked on 7 November 2026.** The command's usage line and options name no currency.

**Done when.** The command takes a currency as it takes a scheme, and the conventions say when a page is inspected in dollars.

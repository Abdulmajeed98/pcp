# 10: A price wider than its line wraps its unit under its figure

**Category:** enhancement

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**What happens.** At a phone's largest text, the browser's text at 200% (WCAG 1.4.4), a Shown Price in dinars of six or seven figures, such as "≈ 6,824,750 IQD", is wider than its line. The cause is that the Shown Price is one left-to-right unit that never breaks inside (`ShownPrice`, `apps/shopper/DESIGN.md`'s Prices), and the sheet clips whatever runs past its edge. Nothing scrolls sideways.

Builder ticket 08's `break-ui` measured, each overflow pulling its line's other columns with it:

- the Build slip's Lowest total at 320 pixels, by up to 73 pixels;

**What the owner chose: option A.** The unit wraps under the figure, and only where the line cannot hold the whole price. The "≈" stays on the figure's line, and the figure stays whole, at its line's size.

As ticket 08 told the owner, every price then fits at 390 pixels. At 320 pixels, a seven-figure figure on its own may still be a little wider than the slip's line and a Vendor's.

7. **S7, the day, the plate's figure and the described-by ids.** Fixed in `e070124`: `dayWritten` (`shared/lib/day-written.ts`) for a Part's last change and the day a rate was set; `PlateFigure` (`shared/ui/plate.tsx`) for an Offer's prices and the rate form's "On the board after Save"; and `describedBy` (`shared/lib/described-by.ts`), the Part form's helper, now also used by the Offer, Vendor, contact details and rate forms.

Every existing test passed unchanged, the films included, under the No Snap Rule: the board's, the Part form's, the edit screens', the Offers' and the new Vendor's, 77 lines in one run before the commit.
9. **S9, the currency list written three times.** Fixed in `9dd3fbc` and `9f05e0f`: `Currency` moves to `domain/currency/currency.ts`, since `browse` and `builder` use it too; the OpenAPI document names the enum `Currency`, the generated client exports it, and the Shopper's `shared/currency` takes its type from `@pcp/api-client`, its list only ordering the codes, IQD first, and failing the type check on a code the API does not know.

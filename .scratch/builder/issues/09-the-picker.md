# 09: The picker

**Spec:** [the builder spec](../spec.md)

**What to build:** From the Build page, a Shopper opens a Category's picker and chooses a Part for their Build. The picker lists the Category's Parts that some Vendor has in stock, lowest price first, with the Category page's search, filters and pages (BLD-2). It hides the Parts that are not Compatible with the Build so far, and a switch shows them all, marking those that are not (BLD-3). The Part already in the Build is marked. Picking a Part puts it in the Build and goes to the Build page. With this ticket a Shopper can assemble a Complete Build on a phone in each language, which makes success criterion 5 true.

**Blocked by:** 04, 08

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] **The page** at `/{language}/build/{category}`, built to DESIGN.md. It is rendered on the server for each request and asks `GET v1/visible-parts` and its filters with in stock only, the Build from the cookie, and the switch when it is on, through `api-client` with no cache. An unknown Category answers the not-found page with a 404 in the page's language.
- [x] **Its query** is the Category page's, read and written by the same functions of the `search-parts` feature, with one more parameter for the switch. The address holds only what is on. The search and the filters are the Category page's form, sent with GET, so they work before any script has loaded. The currency switch drops the price range, as on a Category page, and the language and scheme switches keep the whole query.
- [x] **A Part in the picker** shows its photo or its Category's icon, its name and manufacturer in English, and its Lowest Price through `ShownPrice`. It has a link to its Part page and the pick form from ticket 07.
  - The Part the Build already holds is marked.
  - A Part the switch shows that is not Compatible is marked so, never by colour alone.
  - A RAM kit or a drive the Build holds is marked with how many.
- [x] **What the sheet says** is decided by a plain function, which tells apart:
  - a Category no Vendor has in stock;
  - a search or filters that found nothing, with Clear all;
  - a list where every Part in stock is not Compatible, offering the switch;
  - a page past the last.
- [x] **The preview.** A title in the page's language naming the Category, with no price, and the page marked for search engines not to index.
- [x] **The motion** DESIGN.md gives the picker and the pick, filmed with `film` and judged by `snapsOf`, with reduced motion and without it.
- [x] **The words.** Every new text is in `i18n`'s Shopper set in all three languages, as drafts under LANG-7.
- [x] **`pnpm shopper:inspect`** on the picker of a Category of one Part, of the GPUs' 400, and with the worst-case Build, the switch on and off. Every FAIL is fixed, and every WARN fixed or defended under Comments.

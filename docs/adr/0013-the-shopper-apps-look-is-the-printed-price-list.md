# The Shopper app's look is the printed price list

The Shopper app's look is Taste's The page is the object, drawn as the printed price list a computer shop hands over the counter. Each page is a piece of the same stationery laid on a table: a card over a dim room, printed sheets with scalloped edges, a till slip with a torn foot, a stamp, tear-off tabs, a square plate, a swing tag of filters, a coupon to cut out, a box label with a barcode, and a back cover. On every sheet the names stand at the start of their lines and the prices at the end, in tabular figures, joined by dotted leaders, a Converted Price with its "≈" at the price's own weight. It is set in Big Shoulders over Public Sans, with Noto Kufi Arabic for Kurdish and Arabic; it is printed in bone, chocolate and one green, with a dark scheme of bone ink on chocolate and a switch between the two; its corners are square, its edges cut and nothing casts a shadow; under the paper lie dim photographs of PC hardware, and nothing in it is particular to one city. It has a section on motion of its own, the motion of paper and print, led by Emil Kowalski's skills. [apps/shopper/DESIGN.md](../../apps/shopper/DESIGN.md) records it; the sheet as dealt is in [apps/shopper/design/taste/](../../apps/shopper/design/taste/).

The owner chose it by eye on 6 November 2026, in the `browse` module (`.scratch/browse`, tickets 07 and 08), in their words "the most elegant out of them, but it needs some motion in it", and kept every tweak and every motion its renderings added ("now it is good, and keep all recommendations"). Fit Diagram, the look of 15 October 2026, was drawn before any Shopper page existed. Its idea, a Build drawn like a motherboard manual, belongs to the `builder` module, and on the four kinds of page `browse` builds there is no Build, so it shrank to paper, black print and ruled lines; ticket 07's critique by Taste and Impeccable found it interchangeable with any other site of PC parts, its rules against Taste's house rules and its "≈" quieter than the figure it marks, where the brief puts prices and the "≈" at the centre of the page.

The fonts change in part (below, and ADR-0006). The components are still the Shopper app's own shadcn/ui set on Base UI, created in the Lyra style when the first is needed, whose square corners the new look keeps; their look follows the new DESIGN.md.

## Amended 6 November 2026: the "≈" and a fifth allowance

Building the frame and the home page in browse ticket 09 showed that Noto Kufi Arabic's file for mathematical signs, which its range of characters names for the "≈", holds Arabic mathematical letters and no "≈", so none of the three faces draws it. The owner answered on 6 November 2026:

- The "≈" is drawn in the device's own sans, which every phone and computer holds, beside the price's figures in Public Sans. The theme declares Noto Kufi Arabic's Arabic file itself rather than importing all five of its package's files, so the mathematical file is no longer fetched: a page with Arabic script downloads 187 KB of fonts on the first visit, with a "≈" or without one, as measured in Chrome on the home page in each language and on the not-found page.
- The sheet allows a fifth of Taste's rules, `still-page`: the paper holds still once printed, so a page with one sheet, as the not-found page is, moves fewer than the eight things Taste's inspector asks for.

## Considered Options

- **Keep Fit Diagram, with its execution fixed.** The critique's three changes on the old look: a title and a way home, prices drawn as the page's centre, a frame of one pair of margins. It would have kept a look made for a page `browse` does not build.
- **The first deal** (Taste's `draw.mjs` with its run for public pages, sector `ecommerce`, 4 November 2026): The contact sheet (`contact-sheet`, seed 253395428), everything at once in strict rows on a dark field; **The page is the object** (`page-is-the-object`, seed 2092360077), chosen; The pinned poster (`pinned-poster`, seed 1959420171), a pinned title over portraits on bubblegum and lime. All three were first drawn with pictures of Erbil; on 5 November the owner kept the second, asked that it be drawn again without a place, and that every picture stand for PCs.
- **The second deal** (6 November 2026, the same brief with no city): The split stage (`split-stage`, seed 289709026), a held photograph beside a scrolling price card on orange and black; The sentence is the layout (`sentence-as-layout`, seed 1777153484, sector `physical-product`), one poster-sized sentence with the pictures printed in one blue; and The page is the object again, drawn over hardware. The owner took none and asked for more creativity.
- **The third deal** (6 November 2026, `--tone bold`, the earlier five avoided): One motif (`one-motif`, seed 1795324496), a CPU's pin-1 corner as the one shape on soot and lime; Colour chapters (`colour-chapters`, seed 1987852394), a coolant line through every headline in poster colours; The name as a monument (`name-as-monument`, seed 1208468623), the Part's name set huge across a held photograph on plum-black.

Each was rendered as the home page, a Category page with its filters, a Part page with twenty Offers and a Vendor page, in Kurdish and English, at 360 and 1440 pixels, in both schemes, filled with the made-up shop of ticket 06, and shown to the owner in the built-in browser; the third deal's and the chosen direction's moved live. The owner then chose the first deal's The page is the object as the most elegant, with seven tweaks that push the printed object further and seven motions of paper and print, rendered for that choice.

## Consequences

- ADR-0007's sentence that the Shopper app's look is Fit Diagram is replaced; the rest of ADR-0007 stands.
- ADR-0006's naming of the Shopper app's fonts, Archivo and Noto Kufi Arabic, and its figure for what a Shopper page downloads, are replaced: Big Shoulders and Public Sans set Latin script in place of Archivo, and Noto Kufi Arabic stays for Kurdish and Arabic. The rest of ADR-0006 stands: each face comes from a Fontsource package in the `ui` library (`@fontsource-variable/big-shoulders` and `@fontsource-variable/public-sans`, both 5.3.0, SIL Open Font License 1.1), served by the app itself, a page downloading only the files its letters need. They are installed with the frame, in browse ticket 09, under pnpm's minimum release age.
- Neither new face draws a letter of Arabic script, so every Kurdish and Arabic word is drawn whole in Noto Kufi Arabic and none changes font mid-word, as ADR-0006 requires. The "≈" is in neither Latin face's files, and is drawn from Noto Kufi Arabic's file for mathematical signs in every language.
- By the packages' listings, a page with Arabic script on it downloads about 187 KB of fonts on the first visit, where it downloaded about 214 KB: Big Shoulders' Latin file is 36 KB and Public Sans' 27 KB, against Archivo's 90 KB with its width axis, and Noto Kufi Arabic's Arabic file stays 124 KB. A page that shows a "≈" adds Noto Kufi Arabic's mathematical file, 22 KB. The sizes are measured again when the faces are installed.
- The theme in `@pcp/ui/themes/shopper.css` is rewritten with the new tokens of both schemes and the motion's curves, and the frame is rebuilt to the new look, with the three fixes the owner took on 5 November 2026: a way home in the name slot, a plain description until the platform's name is chosen; three switches, the currency drawn as the price's own control, the language, and light, dark or the device's, each working without script; and a not-found page that lists the eight Categories (browse ticket 09). Then the four kinds of page are built to it (tickets 09 to 13).
- The photographs are of PC hardware, under licences that allow reuse, credited on the page that shows them, and never of a place, a maker's logo or a person who could be known. A Part keeps its own photo, or its Category's icon.
- Every Shopper page is inspected with Taste's rules for public pages, with the four allowances the owner gave the sheet: `few-pictures`, `eyebrow`, `off-centre` and `palette-cream`, and a fifth, `still-page`, given on 6 November 2026 (above).
- Taste's sheet breaks hard rules as dealt (Big Shoulders in capitals, 13- and 12-pixel steps, a light scheme only, faces that draw no Arabic script, a coupon that is a form) and the Shopper app holds each, as `apps/shopper/design/taste/held-rules.md` records.
- Every movement is CSS, on `transform`, `opacity` and `clip-path`, once a page view and never on a list drawn again, with the finished page under reduced motion; no animation library is added.
- The root DESIGN.md changes with it, as the owner decided on 6 November 2026 (browse ticket 08): a look's display face may take sizes of its own in `rem`, each at least 1.375rem (22 pixels), and a look may space its own pieces beyond 48 pixels, in 4-pixel steps. The shared text sizes stay as they are, and the Shopper app keeps them, a 16-pixel body and 18 pixels for an item's name and price; its Big Shoulders takes four sizes from 22 to 88 pixels, and its printed pieces spaces of 56 to 140 pixels. The dashboard's rules are otherwise unchanged.

# The Platform Rate without a city

The glossary names the market the Platform Rate follows. CONTEXT.md says the rate is "set by hand to follow the Erbil market rate".

## Why this is out of scope

The glossary defines the platform as it is, not as it is planned, so that every screen, test and conversation means the same thing by a term. Today the API has one city, Erbil, and the Admin follows its market, so the definition names it. A definition that named no city would describe a platform that does not exist.

Taking the platform beyond Erbil is the owner's plan, and the design already names no city: its photographs stand for PCs, not places. The rate is different, since it is a fact about a market and not a look. A second city will change what the Platform Rate is, to one rate for every city or one for each, and the definition changes with that decision, made with the cities it serves, not before it.

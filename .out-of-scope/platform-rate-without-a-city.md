# The Platform Rate without a city

The glossary keeps the Erbil market in the Platform Rate's definition. CONTEXT.md says the rate is "set by hand to follow the Erbil market rate", and that stays while Erbil is the only city a Vendor can be in.

## Why this is out of scope

The glossary states the domain as it is. Erbil is the API's only city, so the market the Admin follows is Erbil's, and a definition that named no city would describe a platform that does not exist yet.

Taking the platform beyond Erbil is the owner's plan, and the design already names no city: its photographs stand for PCs, not places. The rate is different, since it is a fact about a market and not a look. When a second city comes, the Platform Rate's definition changes with it, to one rate for every city or one for each, and that is a decision of its own, made then with the cities it serves.
